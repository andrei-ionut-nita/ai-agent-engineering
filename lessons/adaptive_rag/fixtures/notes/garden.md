# Garden Beds

The garden at the back of the house has three raised beds. The first
bed grows tomatoes and basil together, since basil is said to improve
tomato flavor when planted nearby. The second bed is dedicated
entirely to root vegetables like carrots and beets. The third bed
rotates whatever seeds are left over from the season.

Watering happens early in the morning, before the sun is high, so
less water is lost to evaporation. The tomato bed needs the most
attention: it's watered daily in summer, while the root vegetable bed
only needs watering every two or three days.

Wind speed matters for the garden too: a stretch of days above 20
km/h dries out the raised beds noticeably faster than the weather
station's average readings would suggest, since the beds sit in a
funnel between two fences.
