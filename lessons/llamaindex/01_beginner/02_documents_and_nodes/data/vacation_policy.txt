Nimbus Robotics: Vacation and Time Off Policy

Full-time employees at Nimbus Robotics accrue 20 days of paid vacation
per calendar year, credited monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month.
Unused vacation days roll over into the next year, up to a maximum
carryover of 10 days. Anything beyond that cap is forfeited on
January 1st unless local law requires otherwise.

To request time off, submit a request through the HR portal at least
five business days in advance for anything longer than two consecutive
days. Same-day sick leave does not require advance notice, just a
message to your manager before your normal start time.

New hires begin accruing vacation from their first day, but cannot use
more than 5 days during their first 90 days of employment, matching the
standard onboarding probation period.

Nimbus Robotics also observes 10 paid public holidays per year, and
offers 12 weeks of paid parental leave for a new child, whether by
birth, adoption, or fostering.
