Nimbus Robotics: Expense Reimbursement Policy

Business expenses under 100 EUR can be submitted directly through the
finance portal with a photo of the receipt, and are typically
reimbursed within 5 business days. Expenses over 100 EUR need
pre-approval from your manager before you spend the money, not after.

Travel booked for client visits or conferences should go through the
company travel portal so it's billed directly to Nimbus Robotics rather
than reimbursed after the fact. Meals during approved business travel
are reimbursed up to 60 EUR per day without requiring itemized
receipts, only a total.

Software subscriptions and small hardware purchases under 200 EUR
needed for a specific project can be expensed directly by the
engineering team lead without further approval, provided they're
logged in the team's tooling spreadsheet for budget tracking.

Personal cell phone bills are not reimbursed. Employees whose role
requires being reachable outside office hours instead receive a fixed
30 EUR monthly stipend added to payroll.
