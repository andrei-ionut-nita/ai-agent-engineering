Nimbus Robotics: Engineering Handbook (excerpt)

Code review: every pull request needs at least one approval before
merging, two approvals for changes touching the robot's motion-control
firmware. Reviews should focus on correctness and safety first, style
second, since the linter already enforces formatting.

On-call rotation: the robotics platform team runs a weekly on-call
rotation covering the fleet dashboard and the robots' remote update
service. On-call engineers carry a pager and are expected to acknowledge
a page within 15 minutes during business hours, 30 minutes overnight.

Deploys: firmware updates to robots in active warehouses only go out on
Tuesdays and Wednesdays, never on a Friday, and always to a single
warehouse first as a canary before a wider rollout.

Testing: any change to the navigation stack requires a passing run in
the physical test warehouse at the Cluj office, not just simulation,
before it can be approved for firmware release.
