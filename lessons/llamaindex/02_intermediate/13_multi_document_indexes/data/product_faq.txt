Nimbus Robotics: Product FAQ (Cobalt-1 Warehouse Robot)

What is the Cobalt-1? The Cobalt-1 is Nimbus Robotics' autonomous
warehouse picking robot. It navigates aisles using LIDAR and a
downward-facing camera, and can carry loads up to 25 kilograms on its
top shelf.

How long does the battery last? A fully charged Cobalt-1 runs for
approximately 8 hours of continuous picking work, and recharges to 80%
in 45 minutes on its docking station.

Does it need special flooring? No, the Cobalt-1 works on any flat
concrete or epoxy warehouse floor. It does require aisle widths of at
least 1.2 meters to turn safely.

What happens if it loses connectivity? The Cobalt-1 stops in place and
enters a safe-hold state if it loses network connectivity for more than
10 seconds, resuming its task automatically once reconnected.

How many units can one operator supervise? A single operator using the
Nimbus fleet dashboard can supervise up to 12 Cobalt-1 units running
simultaneously in one warehouse.
