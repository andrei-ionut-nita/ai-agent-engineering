# Home Aquarium

A 40-gallon freshwater tank on the kitchen counter, across from where
the sourdough starter sits. It houses six cardinal tetras, two
bristlenose plecos, and a small colony of cherry shrimp, along with
java fern and anubias attached to a piece of driftwood.

Water changes are 25% weekly, always with dechlorinated water matched
to the tank's temperature before adding it back. Filter media (the
sponge, not the ceramic rings) gets rinsed in old tank water every
other water change, never under the tap, since tap water's chlorine
kills the beneficial bacteria living in it.

The plecos are nocturnal and mostly stay hidden behind the driftwood
during the day, which is normal and not a sign anything is wrong.
