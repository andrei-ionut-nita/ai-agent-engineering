# Japanese Study Journal

Studying toward the JLPT N3 exam, roughly eighteen months in. Daily
routine is 20 minutes of spaced-repetition vocabulary review first
thing in the morning, then 30 minutes of grammar or reading practice
in the evening, five days a week, with weekends reserved for a longer
session (an hour or more) working through a graded reader.

Kanji is the current weak point: recognition while reading is fine,
but recall while writing from memory lags well behind it, especially
for anything above the N4 list. The current fix is a dedicated
handwriting-only review pass once a week, separate from the daily
spaced-repetition deck, specifically to force recall instead of
recognition.
