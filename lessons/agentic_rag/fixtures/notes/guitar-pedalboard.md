# Guitar Pedalboard

The board lives in the den, next to the record shelves. Signal chain,
in order from the guitar: tuner, compressor, overdrive, fuzz,
modulation (chorus), delay, reverb, then into the amp. Fuzz goes after
overdrive and before modulation deliberately, putting fuzz any later
in the chain makes it sound thin and buzzy instead of thick.

Power comes from a single isolated-output supply, not a daisy chain,
after a daisy-chained delay pedal picked up an audible hum from a
digital pedal sharing its power line. Cables get an ohms check with a
multimeter every few months; a bad cable is still the most common
reason a "dead" signal chain turns out to have nothing wrong with any
of the pedals themselves.
