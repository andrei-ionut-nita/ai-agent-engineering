# Sourdough Starter

The starter (nicknamed "Clarence") lives on the kitchen counter, next
to the aquarium, in a half-liter glass jar with a loose-fitting lid.
It was started from scratch in January 2024 using just flour and
water, no store-bought starter culture.

At room temperature, Clarence needs feeding every 12 hours, equal
parts flour and water by weight. If it's going in the fridge for
longer than a few days, once a week is enough, since the cold slows
the yeast down. Skipping a feeding at room temperature for more than a
day makes the starter smell sharply of acetone, a sign it's hungry,
not a sign it's dead.

Baking day starts the night before: pull the starter, feed it, and
wait for it to double in size (usually 4 to 6 hours) before mixing the
dough.
