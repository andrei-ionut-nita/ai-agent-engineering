# Vinyl Collection

About 240 records, shelved in the den, sorted first by genre (jazz,
soul, soundtrack, everything else), then alphabetically by artist
within each genre. The den stays climate-controlled year-round
specifically because warped records are almost always a heat-damage
problem, not a handling problem.

The turntable is a belt-drive model bought secondhand in 2022; the
belt gets replaced roughly once a year, sooner if the platter's speed
starts sounding slightly off during playback. Records get an
anti-static brush pass before every play, no exceptions, since dust
ground into the grooves during playback is what actually causes
long-term wear, not the stylus itself.
