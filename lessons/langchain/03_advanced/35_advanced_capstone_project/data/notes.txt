Project Aurora is a personal weather station built from a Raspberry Pi
and a set of outdoor sensors. It logs temperature, humidity, and wind
speed every five minutes to a local database. The project started in
March 2024 as a way to learn embedded electronics.

The garden at the back of the house has three raised beds. The first
bed grows tomatoes and basil together, since basil is said to improve
tomato flavor when planted nearby. The second bed is dedicated entirely
to root vegetables like carrots and beets. The third bed rotates
whatever seeds are left over from the season.

Recipe notes: the best pizza dough recipe found so far uses 00 flour,
a 48-hour cold ferment in the refrigerator, and a very small amount of
yeast, only half a teaspoon per batch. Baking at the highest oven
setting, with a preheated steel, gives the best crust.
