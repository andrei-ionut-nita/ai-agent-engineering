# Household Maintenance Log

**June 3rd** - Mia recalibrated the greenhouse's drifting humidity
sensor after Dev's group-chat post about it. Turned out not to be a
dead sensor at all, just a loose connector letting moisture creep in
over the winter. Mia pulled the multimeter from the garage bench's top
drawer to confirm the sensor itself still read correctly once reseated,
rather than replacing a perfectly good part. Took about twenty minutes
total, most of it spent finding where the connector had worked loose.

**June 20th** - Mia replaced the batteries in both smoke detectors
upstairs, routine, nothing notable.

**July 8th** - Dev cleared a clogged gutter downspout on the north side
of the house after a heavy storm the night before.
