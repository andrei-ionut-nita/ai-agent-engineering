# Living Room Book Club

Priya hosts a small book club in the living room every other Thursday,
currently partway through "The Overstory," which has led to more
tangents about trees and root systems than actual chapter discussion
most weeks.

Before the last meeting, the lamp behind the reading chair started
flickering and then died entirely partway through setting up chairs.
Priya traced it to a frayed cord rather than the bulb, and borrowed the
soldering station from the garage bench to patch the wire back together
instead of buying a whole new lamp. It held up fine through the
meeting, though Priya mentioned wanting to redo the splice properly
with heat-shrink tubing at some point rather than leaving it wrapped in
electrical tape.

Next up for the group, once "The Overstory" wraps, is likely something
shorter, since this book ran long for a group that meets only twice a
month.
