# Garage Workshop

Mia's woodworking workshop takes up the back half of the garage, built
around a table saw she rebuilt from a secondhand parts kit two winters
ago. Lumber gets stacked along the near wall to air-dry before use,
since the humidity in the garage swings a lot between seasons.

To keep an eye on that, Mia has been logging wood moisture readings by
hand every few days, jotting them in a notebook clipped to the drying
rack. She started the log after a batch of walnut boards warped on her
partway through a cabinet build, and traced it back to using lumber
that hadn't dried enough. The notebook now has almost a year of
readings, enough that she's been able to tell newly delivered lumber
needs roughly six weeks on the rack before it is safe to cut.

The other half of the garage, past a half-wall of pegboard, holds a
shared electronics bench that Mia's housemate Dev also uses.
