# Backyard Greenhouse

Dev's greenhouse sits at the end of the yard and currently holds three
rows of peppers, started from seed back in February. A small humidity
sensor hangs from the center rafter, reporting readings to a spreadsheet
Dev checks most mornings before work.

Sometime in late May, Dev noticed the humidity sensor's readings had
started drifting noticeably high, showing eighty percent humidity on
mornings that felt closer to sixty in the greenhouse itself. Dev flagged
the drift in the household group chat, mostly to ask if anyone had a
spare sensor lying around, since ordering a replacement felt wasteful
if the problem turned out to be something simple.

Watering is still done by hand with a can, twice a day in the warmer
months, which Dev has been meaning to automate for a while, since
forgetting the evening round on a hot day stresses the peppers badly
enough to show up in the next day's growth.
