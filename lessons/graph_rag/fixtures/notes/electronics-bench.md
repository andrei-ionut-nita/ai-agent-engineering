# Garage Electronics Bench

Past the pegboard divider in the garage, on the side opposite Mia's
table saw, sits a small electronics bench that Mia and Dev both use for
whatever project needs soldering or wiring that week. It holds a
soldering station, a small parts bin of resistors and headers, a spare
Raspberry Pi that gets passed between projects rather than living
inside any one of them permanently, and a multimeter that's older than
either of them has lived in the house.

The multimeter in particular gets borrowed constantly, mostly for
checking whether a sensor is actually dead or just wired wrong, and it
lives in the top drawer closest to the soldering station so it's never
buried under anything else.

Dev keeps a small tray of jumper wires on the bench too, sorted loosely
by length, left over from an earlier project that never got finished.
