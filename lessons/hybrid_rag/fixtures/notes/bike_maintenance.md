# Bike Maintenance Notes

Gear shifting had gotten sloppy over the last month or two, skipping
one or two cogs under load on hills instead of moving cleanly one gear
at a time. It felt like a cable stretch problem, and it was, but not
the derailleur cable itself.

The actual fix was regreasing and re-torquing the rear derailleur
hanger bolt, which had loosened just enough to let the whole derailleur
flex sideways under pedaling load. It takes a 5mm hex and should be
torqued to 8 Nm, not just snugged down by feel. The chain also got a
fresh application of Squirt Dry Lube afterward, since the old lube had
picked up enough dust and grit over the winter to grind rather than
lubricate.

Shifting has been clean and precise since, even standing and mashing
up the steeper hills on the usual loop.
