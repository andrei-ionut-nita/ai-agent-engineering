# Espresso Machine Notes

Shots were pulling sour and thin for weeks no matter how the grind was
adjusted, which usually means old, stale scale buildup inside the
boiler rather than anything wrong with the beans or the grinder. A
descale finally fixed it.

The descaling solution used was Puly Caff Plus, mixed at the ratio on
the bottle, run through a full cycle, then followed by two full water
rinses to clear any residue before pulling another shot. The basket in
use is the machine's stock double basket, rated for an 18 gram dose.

After descaling, the shots came back rich and syrupy again within the
first two pulls, confirming it really was mineral buildup muting the
flavor rather than a grinder or bean problem.
