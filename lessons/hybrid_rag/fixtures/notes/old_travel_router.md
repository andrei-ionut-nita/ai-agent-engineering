# Old Travel Router Notes

The small travel router that used to live in the overnight bag, a
Netgear Nighthawk M1, still works fine but got retired from daily use
once the house's main network was overhauled. It's kept around purely
as a backup for trips, plugged in and reflashed maybe once a year.

Its last known-good firmware was build 20210830, and it's staying
there on purpose. A firmware update two versions later than that one
introduced a bug where the router would drop its cellular connection
entirely after about six hours of uptime, requiring a full power cycle
to recover. Since this router is only ever used away from home, where
a silent drop is a much bigger problem than at home, it's been left on
the older, known-stable build rather than updated.

It still gets packed for every trip, just in case the hotel WiFi turns
out to be unreliable.
