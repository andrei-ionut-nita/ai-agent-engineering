# Houseplant Notes

The fiddle leaf fig by the east window kept dropping leaves from the
bottom up, one or two a week, even though it was getting watered on
schedule and plenty of light. Overwatering was the first guess, but the
soil was drying out fine between waterings, so that wasn't it.

The actual cause turned out to be a nitrogen shortfall. Switching to
FoliGrow 9-3-6 fertilizer, applied every two weeks instead of monthly,
stopped the leaf drop within about three weeks and pushed out several
new leaves at the top, which is usually the clearest sign a plant has
stopped merely surviving and started actually growing again.

The pothos on the same windowsill never showed any of these symptoms,
which lines up with pothos being a much lighter feeder than a fiddle
leaf fig overall.
