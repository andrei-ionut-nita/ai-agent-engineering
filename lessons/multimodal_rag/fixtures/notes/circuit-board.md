# Soldering Practice Board

A perfboard practice project for learning through-hole soldering: a
handful of LEDs, resistors, and a 555 timer chip wired into a simple
blinking-light circuit. It's the third practice board attempted, the
first two had cold solder joints that failed within a week.

The trick that finally fixed the cold-joint problem was heating both
the pad and the component lead together before feeding in solder,
instead of melting solder directly onto the iron tip and dabbing it on,
which never got the joint hot enough to bond properly.

A scope measurement taken during testing is only written down in the
paper notebook, later scanned to a PDF rather than retyped digitally,
the digital note here never got updated with what the scope showed.

Next planned project is a kit-built audio amplifier, once through-hole
soldering feels reliable enough to trust on something more expensive
than a bag of LEDs.
