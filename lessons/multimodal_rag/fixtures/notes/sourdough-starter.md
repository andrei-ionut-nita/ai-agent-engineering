# Sourdough Starter

The starter (nicknamed "Clyde") has been fed daily since January 2025,
equal parts starter, water, and flour by weight, discarding the excess
before each feed. It lives on the kitchen counter in a tall glass jar,
away from direct sun.

A photo of the jar is taped into the kitchen recipe binder to save
guessing at fill levels every morning, two rubber bands mark exactly
where the starter should sit right after a feed and right after a
discard. The note in the binder just says "see jar photo for levels."

Clyde rises fastest in summer, roughly doubling in four hours; in
winter it can take closer to eight. If it hasn't at least doubled by
the next morning, that's the signal to feed again before baking with
it.
