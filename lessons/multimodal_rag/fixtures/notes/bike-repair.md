# Bike Repair Log

The commuter bike (a secondhand steel-frame hybrid) gets a full
tune-up every spring and a quick check after any fall or hard bump to
the rear wheel. The chain and cassette get replaced together roughly
every 3,000 km, replacing one without the other wears the new part
against an already-stretched old one.

A close-up photo of the rear derailleur hanger is kept in the repair
folder, taken the day a shop mechanic pointed out the torque spec
stamped right on the part in red ink, easy to miss unless you're
looking directly at it. The repair log entry that day just says
"hanger bolt done to spec, see photo."

Cable housing gets replaced whenever shifting starts feeling vague
rather than crisp, usually once a year given how much this bike is
ridden in wet weather.
