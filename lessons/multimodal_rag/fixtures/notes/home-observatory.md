# Home Observatory

The home observatory is a small Newtonian reflector telescope set up on
the back deck, on a motorized equatorial mount so it can track a target
across the sky as the Earth rotates. It lives under a folding tarp cover
between sessions, since the deck has no permanent roof.

Two photos from the setup log are worth keeping alongside this note:
one showing the finder scope's current alignment, one showing how the
mount's two tracking motors are wired. Both are referenced from the
maintenance checklist taped inside the equipment box, but the checklist
itself just says "check fig. 1" and "check fig. 2", it doesn't repeat
what's in either photo.

First light was in October 2024. The most common problem after moving
the whole rig has been the finder scope drifting out of alignment with
the main tube; a quick daytime check against a distant chimney usually
catches it before a night session is wasted on it.
