# Closed Terrarium

The terrarium is a sealed glass jar planted with moss, a small fittonia,
and a few pothos cuttings, running as a closed miniature ecosystem since
June 2025, watered exactly once at planting and never since. Condensation
on the glass each morning and clearing by afternoon is the sign the
water cycle inside is balanced correctly.

If the glass stays fogged all day, that means too much water went in
at the start and the lid should be cracked open for a few hours to let
some humidity escape. If no condensation ever forms, it's under-watered
and needs a small, one-time top-up.

It sits on a north-facing windowsill, bright but with no direct sun,
which would overheat a sealed jar this size within an afternoon.
