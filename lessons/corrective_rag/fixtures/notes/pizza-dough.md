# Pizza Dough Recipe

The best pizza dough recipe found so far uses 00 flour, a 48-hour cold
ferment in the refrigerator, and a very small amount of yeast, only
half a teaspoon per batch. Baking at the highest oven setting, with a
preheated steel, gives the best crust.

The dough is mixed by hand for about ten minutes, until it passes the
"windowpane test": a small piece stretches thin enough to see light
through it without tearing. After that, it rests at room temperature
for one hour before going into the refrigerator for the long,
cold ferment.

Basil from the garden's tomato bed is the usual topping, added fresh
after baking rather than before, so it doesn't wilt and blacken in the
high heat.
