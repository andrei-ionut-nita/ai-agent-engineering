Northwind Trading Co. — Employee Handbook

Section 1: Welcome

Welcome to Northwind Trading Co. This handbook explains the policies
that apply to every employee, from your first day through the rest of
your time here. Read it once in full, then keep it as a reference.

Section 2: Working Hours

Standard working hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM,
with a one hour unpaid lunch break. Employees may request a flexible
schedule through their manager, subject to team coverage needs.

Section 3: Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid vacation per year, plus 8
paid holidays. Sick leave is separate and accrues at one day per
month worked, up to 12 days per year.

Section 4: Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners
with respect. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated and should be
reported to Human Resources immediately.

Section 5: Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses over $25 require a receipt. Submit expense reports
within 30 days of the purchase through the internal finance portal.
